# Dispatch Support

## What Dispatch is

Dispatch is a news reader for iPhone. It brings the publications and newsletters you choose into one place, fetches each story from its publisher on your iPhone, and sets it in a calm, paginated reader. It's free, needs no account and shows no ads of its own. Signing in to publishers you already subscribe to is optional.

## What it runs on

- **iPhone** with iOS 26 or later.
- **Summary, 5 Facts and 5 W's** need a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Elsewhere they show "Apple Intelligence isn't available on this device."
- **Apple Watch:** watchOS 26.2 or later.
- **Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets**, **CarPlay**, **Open in Dispatch News** in the share sheet and the **Open in Dispatch News** button in Safari are included.
- **Siri:** ask "What's the latest on Dispatch?", "Give me my Dispatch briefing" or "Play my saved articles on Dispatch". The Shortcuts app lists every command.

## Get in touch

Email dispatch.news@icloud.com with a question, a bug or an idea. Dispatch is made by Liam Walsh.

You can also write from inside the app: **Settings › About › Send Feedback**, or **Found a bug? Tell me.** Each opens an email in your mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Publishers: to have your content taken out of Dispatch, write to the same address.

## Common questions

**How do I erase what Dispatch has stored?** Settings › Privacy & Data › Delete All Data. The [Privacy Policy](https://liamwalsh-redirect.github.io/dispatch/privacy) lists what it removes and what it doesn't.

**A paywalled story shows only its first part.** Sign in to that publisher with your own subscription under Settings › Sources › Log in to Sources.
